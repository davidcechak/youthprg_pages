About Youth PRG

Empowering young people through international experiences and opportunities.

More about us

Our core mission is to empower young individuals primarily in AI literacy, digital literacy, entrepreneurship/employability, media literacy sustainability and healthy lifestyle. Our staff possess considerable expertise and practical know-how in these areas, having actively contributed to the implementation of numerous KA1 and KA2 projects focused on these critical topics.

We aim to achieve both local impact through the implementation of mobilities and other activities within the Olomouc region, while also operating on a European scale. This broader reach is facilitated by our robust network of international partnerships, including our significant collaboration as a member of Youth HUB, which amplifies our collective efforts.

Our primary target group is youth aged 18-25, with a particular focus on marginalized youth. Our initiatives are designed to address their specific needs and challenges through targeted educational programs and activities, and to fill the gap in important life skills that traditional education fails to provide.

Volunteers

Beyond our core team, Youth Prague z.s. benefits significantly from a dedicated group of active volunteers. These individuals contribute their time and diverse skills to support various aspects of our projects, from logistical coordination and event support to content creation and outreach. Their commitment amplifies our operational capacity and extends our reach, playing a vital role in the successful execution of our activities and enhancing our overall impact within the community.
