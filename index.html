About us

Our Projects

We create and implement international projects within Erasmus+, including youth exchanges, training courses, and cooperation projects such as small-scale and cooperation partnerships. We also collaborate on regional initiatives, including Visegrad Fund programmes.

Who We Are

Established in 2023, Youth PRG is a youth-led organisation helping young people grow through Erasmus+ opportunities and international learning experiences.
