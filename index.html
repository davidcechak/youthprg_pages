About us

Our Projects

We create and implement international projects within Erasmus+, including youth exchanges, training courses and long-term partnership initiatives. We also collaborate on regional projects such as Visegrad Fund programmes.

Who We Are

Established in 2023, Youth PRG is a youth-led organisation helping young people grow through Erasmus+ opportunities and international learning experiences.
